Theme Image-Derived Colorways

These palettes are pulled from the reference images in the Themes folder. Treat them as visual directions for alphas, modifiers, accents, and novelty colors rather than exact manufacturer color codes. Real versions of these named sets usually sit in the high-end budget range; lower or mid-tier matches are typically clones or inspired alternatives.
