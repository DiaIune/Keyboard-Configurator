Keycap Profile Chart

Sculpted profiles guide each row with different heights and angles. Uniform profiles keep rows level, which helps unusual layouts but changes the finger feel.

SA / KAT
Tall / Sculpted / Spherical
SA is tall and vintage-sounding; KAT is smoother and lower but still rounded and sculpted.
KAM / XDA / MOA
Low-medium / Uniform / Broad top
Uniform profiles are flexible for compact and unusual layouts. MOA is more rounded, but this category covers the broad uniform feel.
OEM
Medium / Sculpted / Standard flat top
The standard prebuilt feel, taller than Cherry and broadly comfortable.
MTNU
Medium / Sculpted / Spherical
Modern short sculpted profile from Matt3o/GMK lineage; cupped but less towering than SA/MT3.
Cherry
Low / Sculpted / Standard flat top
The enthusiast default: shorter, familiar, and widely available across GMK, PBTfans, JTK, Domikey, Milkyway, NicePBT, and many others.
