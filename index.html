Availability changes quickly, so the page links to product families and search terms rather than claiming every color/layout is always in stock.

  • PBTfans Absolute is described by KBDfans as using a warm beige GMK L9 base with geometric, free-floating color compositions: KBDfans PBTfans Absolute.
  • KBDfans Tofu60 Redux listings show a wide aluminum/acrylic color range including white, black, gray, blue, green, purple, pink, lemon, champagne, acrylic, and raw acrylic: KBDfans Tofu60 Redux.
  • Keycap profile summaries use common distinctions found in current guides: sculpted versus uniform, low Cherry and KAM/XDA/MOA, medium OEM/MTNU, and tall spherical SA/KAT-style families: Keychron profile guide and MKB Guide.
  • Keycap recommendations include GMK, Domikey, JTK, DCS/Signature Plastics, Keyreative, Milkyway, PBTfans, NicePBT, and CannonCaps, with vendor anchors including NovelKeys, CannonKeys, Divinikey, and Unikeys.
  • CannonKeys' current keycap collections list broad in-stock sets across GMK, NicePBT, CannonCaps, Signature Plastics, KAT, DCS, and CXA: CannonKeys in-stock keycaps.
  • Divinikey carries brand examples such as JTK sets, including Flight and Brittle colorways, plus KAP sets such as Grey Alert; these examples help map dark/navy, translucent dark, and industrial gray/red color directions: Divinikey keycap sets.
  • NovelKeys currently surfaces GMK and DCS options, and its Classic-TKL references ABS case construction plus black, beige, yellow, blue, orange, clear, pink, and translucent variants: NovelKeys GMK and Classic-TKL info.
  • Board recommendations reference current/common families such as Keychron V/Q/Q Ultra, MonsGeek M series, Qwertykeys Neo, KBDfans Tofu, NovelKeys Classic-TKL, Weikav, Wooting, Mode, Geon, and CannonKeys, filtered by layout, material, and color direction.
